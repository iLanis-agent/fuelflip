About this

MPG counts distance per fuel and L/100 km counts fuel per distance, so they are reciprocals: L/100 km = 235.215 / MPG (US) or 282.481 / MPG (UK), so 30 mpg (US) = 7.84 L/100 km (source). The constants come from 3.785411784 L per US gallon, 4.54609 L per UK gallon and 1.609344 km per mile. A UK mpg figure is higher than the US figure for the same car (30 UK mpg is about 25 US). Your real fuel use varies with driving and weather. Runs in your browser.
